# SANE Procedure: Tester

For workstream assignments:

1. Read the relevant requirements of the Verification Spec named in your prompt
   and the Job Specs and Job Reports of the listed jobs. Those jobs provide
   context; verify only the behaviors or suites assigned to you.
2. Keep workstream documents read-only except for a Test Report explicitly
   assigned to you. Verification Crew owns the shared checkpoint Test Report
   when coordinating verification; its Tester children do not edit it.
3. Keep job IDs, checkpoint labels, and workstream-document references out of
   tests and fixtures.
